Brompton SC Club Rankings

Coach & swimmer reference guide

Overview

This app shows personal best (PB) times for all Brompton SC swimmers, grouped by age and gender. Times are sourced from swimmingresults.org and cover both Short Course (SC, 25m) and Long Course (LC, 50m) pools.

Coaches can compare swimmers across strokes and events, track improvement since the last data refresh, see who is approaching qualification standards, and generate an Arena Shortlist.

Filters

The filter panel is shown by default. Use the Hide Filters button (top right of the filter bar) to collapse it and give more space to the table. Click Show Filters to bring it back.

Gender

Toggle between Female, Male, or Both. The age pill counts update automatically to reflect the selected gender.

Age

Each pill represents a year of birth (YOB). The number in brackets shows how many swimmers in that group match the current gender filter. Select one or more to narrow the view. All and None shortcuts are also available.

Strokes

Tick one or more stroke families to show or hide their columns: Back, Free, Fly, Breast, IM. IM covers 100m, 200m, and 400m events.

Course
  • SC — Short Course PB (25m pool)
  • LC — Long Course PB (50m pool)
  • LC→SC — Long Course time converted to SC equivalent using standard factors
  • Best — Fastest of SC, LC, and LC→SC. Hidden by default; tick to reveal. Useful for overall ranking regardless of course.

The Best cell shows the source in small italic text (SC / LC / →SC) so you know which pool the time came from.

Columns

Each stroke has up to four columns (SC, LC, LC→SC, Best). Columns can be sorted by clicking the header — click again to reverse. The Posn column updates to show rank among visible swimmers.

Qualification Indicators (2027)

Where a time meets a qualification standard, one or more coloured badges appear below the time:

BadgeMeaning
RQRegional Qualifier — meets the Regional Championships qualifying time
RCRegional Consideration — meets the Regional Championship consideration standard
CQCounty Qualifier — meets the County Championships qualifying time
CCCounty Consideration — meets the County Championship consideration standard

Standards are age-group specific (based on year of birth) and reflect the qualification age for the 2027 championships — i.e. how old each swimmer will be when competing next season. Qualification is assessed against times swum in 2026.

Qualification times shown are the current published standards. Times for the 2027 season will be updated here once new standards are published by Middlesex County and London Region.

Gap to Next Standard

If a swimmer has not yet reached their next qualifying standard, a label like -1.95s → CQ appears below the date. This shows exactly how much faster they would need to swim to hit that standard. When they reach it, the label disappears and the badge appears instead.

To see the full breakdown of all four standards for that event, tap the time cell to open the History Chart — the qual gap summary is shown at the top of the popup for both SC and LC.

These labels can be hidden with the Hide markers button in the status bar.

History Chart

Tap or click any time cell to open the swimmer's full history chart for that event and course. The popup shows:

  • Time-series chart — every recorded swim plotted chronologically, with horizontal reference lines for each qualification standard (CC, CQ, RC, RQ)
  • Qual gap summary — SC and LC gaps to each standard, shown at the top of the popup
  • Period filter — buttons to zoom the chart to the last 3M, 6M, 1Y, 2Y, or All time
  • Legend checkboxes — toggle individual qual standard lines on or off
  • Swims table — full list of every recorded swim with date, meet name, time, and a ★ marking the current PB

Close the popup with the × button or tap anywhere outside it.

History data is cached from swimmingresults.org. Swimmers in all age groups (2009–2017, both genders) have full history data. If a swimmer's history appears empty, they may have no times recorded on swimmingresults.org yet.

Improvement Markers

A ▲ -0.45s marker appears when a swimmer's most recent recorded swim in that event is their current PB — meaning they set a personal best at their last time competing in that event. The delta shows how much faster they went compared to their previous best.

The marker clears automatically when the swimmer next competes in that event, regardless of outcome. If they go faster, the marker reappears with an updated delta. If they go slower, it stays off until they PB again.

Improvement markers are derived from recorded swim history. A swimmer with no history on swimmingresults.org will not show ▲ markers even if their current PB is recent.

Meet Name

Each time cell shows the date the PB was set. To see the meet name, tap the cell to open the History Chart — the swims table lists every recorded time alongside its date and meet name.

Rank Colouring

Within each visible group (the rows currently shown), the top three times per event are colour coded:

  • Gold — 1st fastest
  • Silver — 2nd fastest
  • Bronze — 3rd fastest

Ranking is recalculated any time filters change or a column is sorted.

Arena Shortlist

The Arena Shortlist button (in the status bar) generates a shortlist of the four fastest swimmers for every 50m event, based on the selected age and gender filters.

To use it:

  1. Set your gender and age filters to the group you want to select from.
  2. Click Arena Shortlist.
  3. The panel shows up to 4 swimmers for each 50m event (50 Back, 50 Free, 50 Fly, 50 Breast), ranked fastest to slowest and grouped by age. The faster of SC and LC→SC is used automatically, with the source shown.
  4. Click Print to send to your printer or save as PDF.
Only 50m events are included in the shortlist — IM and distance events are excluded as Arena races cover sprints only. The shortlist always shows all swimmers for the selected age groups regardless of any active swimmer name filter.
The Arena Shortlist does not constitute Arena team selection. It is a data-driven reference tool based on recorded PBs only. Final selection is at the coach's discretion and may take into account factors such as recent form, training performance, entry limits, relay considerations, and other criteria not reflected in the rankings.

Sorting

Click any course sub-header (e.g. SC under 50 Back) to sort all visible rows by that event time, fastest first. Click again to reverse. The Posn column always reflects the current sort order.

PB Summary

Tap any swimmer's name in the table to open their Personal Best Summary. The popup shows all recorded PBs grouped by stroke (Backstroke, Butterfly, Freestyle, Breaststroke, Individual Medley), with SC and LC columns side by side. Each cell shows the time and the date it was set.

Tap anywhere outside the panel or press Esc to close it.

Swimmer Filter

Type a swimmer's name in the search box (in the filter bar) to filter the table to that person only. An autocomplete list appears as you type. Once selected, a banner appears at the top of the table showing the swimmer's name, age group, and qualification summary.

You can also arrive at a filtered view directly from the home page — select a name from the dropdown and tap View My Results →. The URL includes a ?swimmer= parameter so it can be bookmarked or shared.

Clear the filter with the ✕ button next to the search box, or use the swimmer banner's back to full view link.

Data Source & Freshness

All times shown are the published Swim England personal bests sourced from swimmingresults.org. You can verify any swimmer's recorded times directly at swimmingresults.org/12months/ — search by name to see the last 12 months of published results.

Times are cached locally. The generation timestamp is shown at the top of the rankings page (e.g. "Generated 7 June 2026 09:15 · 175 swimmers"). To request a refresh or correct any data issues, contact Bram Montgomery (bram@bromptonsc.com) or James Mandikos (james.mandikos@gmail.com).
