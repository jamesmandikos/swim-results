Method & caveats

Times pulled live from Swim England (swimmingresults.org). Each projection anchors on the swimmer's best long-course 200 Back — or, where their long-course data is old/thin, their short-course best converted to LC (flagged by a Low confidence). It then applies a damped age-improvement rate (from ~2,000 club progressions) plus the swimmer's own recent trend, projected to today. A guide, not a guarantee: this is an end-of-season meet (not a tapered target meet), so swimmers can land slower than best-case; Summer, Dixie and Freya carry extra uncertainty because their projections lean on converted short-course times or a single old long-course swim. Backstroke starts, turns and race-day form all swing a 200 by seconds.
